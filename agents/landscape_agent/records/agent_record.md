## Test and revision record

Four fresh ChatGPT conversations used the preserved packets and the same evidence. Codex captured the displayed response text without rewriting it. A valid shape does not verify evidence quality or prove that the underlying workflow works.

- **v0.1 primary:** Passed basic validation and selected a digital form. It returned ten competing monitoring signals and embedded a conclusion in the analytical question.
- **v0.1 contrast:** The returned text stopped mid-string and failed JSON parsing. The original is preserved in `responses/contrast_v01_incomplete.txt`.
- **Revision:** v0.2 made the analytical question question-only, capped each finding at 110 words, asked for a shorter response, and required exactly three ranked signals with owners, cadences, observable conditions and actions. It also made the distinction between elicitation and summary fidelity explicit.
- **v0.2 retest:** Both validated, kept the general finding that model output can depart from inputs, and selected different comparators and local metrics. Both returned three triggers and kept each finding under 110 words. Both exceeded the approximate 850-word overall target (1,506 and 1,582 words across JSON string values). The contrast called the template “existing” even though availability was not established. Confirm or prototype it before testing.
- **Threshold review:** The primary response proposed a 10 percentage point coverage improvement. A required-field form can already set a high coverage bar, so useful clarification at equal coverage is a better differentiator. The contrast proposed 15% time savings. Treat that as an unvalidated example and choose a material threshold from baseline effort before testing.
