# Dated landscape evidence register

Checked November 10, 2026. This is a focused evidence set. Scenario details are fictional.

| ID | Original source and publication date | Observation and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | [NIST AI 600-1](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1), July 26, 2024, §2.2 | Voluntary guidance documents confabulation, including errors and divergence from input. It supports checking source fidelity and supplies no local error rate. |
| E2 | [Bank of England and FCA UK financial services AI survey](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/report/2024/artificial-intelligence-in-uk-financial-services-2024), November 21, 2024 | The survey had 118 firm responses. Foundation models accounted for 17% of reported AI use cases. The survey also documents third-party concentration. The denominator is use cases, and the results are historical UK sector context. |
| E3 | [Morgan Stanley Debrief launch](https://www.morganstanley.com/press-releases/ai-at-morgan-stanley-debrief-launch), June 26, 2024 | The firm announced consented meeting notes and summaries with Salesforce integration. Its separately reported 98% of advisor teams applies to its earlier internal knowledge Assistant. This first-party announcement supports an adjacent deployment example, without independent performance or sector prevalence evidence. |
| E4 | [FCA wealth management survey](https://www.fca.org.uk/data/wealth-management-survey-report-2026), August 18, 2026, §3.2 and Annex A | Reports 13% using AI tools and 45% using or considering them. Underlying data ran through December 2024 and banking-led firms were excluded. This is broad AI in a UK wealth sample and is unsuitable for direct trend comparison with E2. |

No matched performance or cost study for either workflow was identified in this register. Jurisdiction, local metrics, and vendor terms are unconfirmed. All proposed thresholds are management choices.
